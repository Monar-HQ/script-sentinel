## Short announcement

Your checkout trusts the scripts it loads. Can you show why?

ScriptSentinel puts script inventory, AST capability signals, integrity checks,
reviewed baselines and change evidence in one local workflow. Start with a
harmless demo, inspect the bytes, review a diff and validate it before approval.

Built by MONAR Services. Ally leads product development and engineering as
co-owner/developer. Luke, owner of unquant.io, provided the AI infrastructure for
the original sandbox research and early development.

The research is attributed, the fixtures are reproducible and the limitations
are visible. Try the demo and tell us where the workflow fails you.

## Demonstration sequence

1. Open the local console and run the bundled harmless checkout demo.
2. Show actual missing integrity metadata and the evidence queue.
3. Review the fixture bytes, approve their baseline and generate a proposal.
4. Validate in a temporary copy, review the diff and demonstrate guarded recovery
   using a disposable copy of the fixture, not a real customer checkout.
5. Change a harmless script in that copy and show the durable drift record.
6. Show Core without provider credentials, then the read-only host-agent contract.

Do not splice historical attack results into a new run or present a ten-fixture
capability benchmark as malware accuracy. Use the actual console screenshots and
`assets/social-v1.png` for presentation. There are no fake customer logos,
testimonials, star counters or universal-protection claims.
