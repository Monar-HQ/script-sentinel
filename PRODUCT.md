# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Store owners checking their first checkout and security engineers reviewing browser
supply chains. They need the same evidence, with progressive detail rather than
different trust rules. The master plan and README establish these audiences.

## Product Purpose

Inventory, explain, compare and verify client-side scripts in authorized local or
staging environments, with optional AI advice. Make the next defensible action clear.

## Brand Personality

Precise, formidable, candid. MONAR black, graphite and signal yellow from the
approved master plan; product clarity takes priority over launch theatrics.

## Anti-references

The existing red-heavy report, fake threat counters, fabricated breach dashboards,
unreadable terminal decoration and unsupported protection percentages.

## Design Principles

Evidence before confidence. Start with a useful demo. Keep approval visible.
Let beginners act and experts inspect. Every displayed result comes from a run.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, semantic landmarks, visible focus, contrast at WCAG AA,
reduced motion, responsive tables and severity text in addition to color.
