## Upstream work

Tree-sitter and its JavaScript grammar provide syntax parsing. Beautiful Soup
parses HTML; HTTPX handles HTTP; Rich supports the CLI; PyYAML supports detection
rule exports. Optional Playwright supports authorized browser observation and
keyring supports OS credential storage. Their licenses remain with their authors.

Incident research is attributed to Optimizely, the CVE Program and the original
researchers in the research pack. Source material is linked, not relicensed.
